## Separate demonstrated understanding from open questions

In the September 12 examples, the learner correctly identified selected input,
parsing failures, schema failures, and unsupported extracted values. On September
19, parsing/schema reasoning transferred to tool arguments, but the differing
order IDs were initially overlooked. The recorded gate was subsequently met
with H2 assistance. Preserve both observations; do not treat the earlier
distinction as never understood or call the gate an unaided pass.

Independent M1 implementation, the Result-handling response, and delayed unaided
transfer were still unobserved in the latest reviewed handoff. These are learning
questions for future work, not evidence of low ability. Typing, speech
transcription, requests for context, and calendar gaps do not measure intelligence,
attention, or practice time. The current journal takes precedence over this snapshot.

## Test the teaching approach

Within existing practice and debrief, observe whether the learner can explain
the mechanism, apply it with less assistance, and later transfer it. Occasionally
ask whether the scenario and pace helped. Change the approach if observations
do not support it; clarity feedback alone does not prove mastery.

The [IES practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1) supports
combining worked examples with practice, linking concrete and abstract ideas,
and revisiting learning over time. Applying those ingredients to this adult
AI course is a teaching judgment, not a validated personalized intervention.
The [Pashler and colleagues review](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/pspi/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x/)
does not support prescribing instruction from a fixed learning-style label.
Both sources were consulted on 2026-10-09.
